Manifesto

Your Mac does what you mean, one key away.

skil.li is a launcher that acts. Type or say what you want: it finds it, or does it, in the app you are in.

  1. 01

    One key away.

    Everything starts from one key: the launcher, or ✦ (Caps Lock, held) with a letter. Your hands stay on the keyboard.

  2. 02

    It does, not only finds.

    Search is where it starts. Then skil.li acts: in the app in front, across your apps, with your tools.

  3. 03

    Fast is the feature.

    The launcher opens in a few milliseconds and keeps up with every keystroke.

  4. 04

    SI only where it earns its place.

    What a rule can answer, a rule answers. A decision model chooses; a writing model writes only when words are needed.

  5. 05

    Your typing stays yours.

    A card never takes a key you meant for the app you are in. An agent's question waits until you stop typing.

  6. 06

    Undo, not questions.

    What can be taken back happens at once, with ⌘Z right after. skil.li asks only before what cannot: sending, deleting for good, paying, overwriting, installing an app.

  7. 07

    One key, one meaning.

    ↩ does, ⌘C copies, ⌘Z undoes, esc goes back. The same key does the same thing everywhere in skil.li.

  8. 08

    The screen is the answer.

    What you see done is not announced, and what you just chose is not confirmed. skil.li speaks only when something failed or happened out of sight, and then says what to do.

  9. 09

    Taught once, then quiet.

    It shows you how the first time, then gets out of the way: no repeated hints, no badges, no nagging.

  10. 10

    Right by default.

    It works the moment it is set up. Settings are for taste, not for making it work.

  11. 11

    What leaves your Mac is listed.

    Only what a request needs leaves your Mac, and only for the provider you chose. Settings → Privacy lists every case, with its switch. No telemetry, no account with us.

  12. 12

    Your own SI, already paid for.

    Your subscription or your API key. No credits, no middleman. Agents act with the rights you give them.

  13. 13

    Free and open, for good.

    Free forever, open source under the GPL. Build on it: a script becomes a command, your tools and MCP servers plug in, and every setting can be changed by you or by your agent, under the same name.

Built in the open. Read it, change it, make it yours.
