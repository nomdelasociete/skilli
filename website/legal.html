Privacy

On this website

The site does not use analytics trackers, advertising pixels or cookies. Fonts and visual assets are served with the site. There is no sign-up, feedback form or visitor profiling.

Technical providers may process connection data, including IP addresses and request information, to deliver and secure the website. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. in the United States. This technical processing is separate from app telemetry.

On your Mac

Your shortcuts, launcher learning, clipboard history, snippets and window layouts stay on your Mac. Provider API keys are stored in macOS Keychain. Voice transcription uses macOS on-device speech recognition. The app’s Settings → Privacy lists each feature that sends anything: what, when, where, with its switch.

When you use SI

SI commands send the text and context needed for the request to the provider you configure. To choose what to do, Ask sends your request and the names it chooses among, such as apps and menu items, to your decision provider. A search by meaning sends short extracts of what it searches. With a TypeSafe key, images and screenshots in your clipboard history are sent once to TypeSafe so search can find them by what they show; a switch in Settings turns this off. A link preview opens the link’s page, without your cookies. Tools you enable may access apps or connected services to carry out an action. Your provider’s privacy policy, terms, pricing and usage limits apply. skil.li does not add an SI subscription.
