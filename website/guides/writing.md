# Your text, anywhere

Fix, translate or rewrite the text you selected, in any app: Mail, Slack,
Notes, a web page's form. skil.li shows the new version, you put it in
place with ↩, and ⌘Z takes it back.

## Fix a sentence

1. Select the text.
2. Press **✦K**, then **G** (Fix Spelling & Grammar).
3. A card shows the new text with the changes highlighted.
4. **↩** puts it in place of your selection. **⌘C** copies it instead,
   **esc** leaves your text as it was.

Changed your mind after ↩? **⌘Z** in your app brings your text back.

## The commands it comes with

| Keys | Command |
| --- | --- |
| ✦K G | Fix Spelling & Grammar |
| ✦K I | Improve Writing |
| ✦K E | Translate to English |
| ✦K R | Translate to French |
| ✦K U · L · T | UPPERCASE · lowercase · Title Case (no SI needed) |
| ✦U | UPPERCASE, in one key |

They are also in the launcher by name, with the selection as their text.

## Not quite right? Say so

In the card, press **⇥** and say what to change: "more formal", "shorter",
"keep the line breaks", "in Spanish". The card shows the new version;
**↩** puts it in place. You can go on until it reads right.

To go further, **⌘↩** continues the conversation in Claude Code or Codex
(Settings → Ask → *Continue in*).

## Write your own commands

A command is a prompt you write once and run on any selection.

1. Type `new command` in the launcher, or open Settings → Commands.
2. Write the prompt. **Insert Variable** puts things in it: the selection
   (`{selection}`), the date, what you copied (`{clipboard}`), the app in
   front. A command can also include what you wrote about yourself in
   **About You** (Settings → Your Details).
3. Choose what happens with the result: **replace** the selection, insert
   it **after**, **copy** it, or show it in a **card**.
4. Optionally: questions it asks before running, a model of its own, the
   tools it may use, and a shortcut.

Commands can be shared: **Copy Link** or **Export File…** in its editor,
and **Import…** in Settings → Commands for someone else's.

## What it uses

Commands write with your SI: your Claude or ChatGPT, set in Settings → SI
Accounts. Only the selected text and your prompt are sent, to the
provider you chose. The case changes (UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case)
run on your Mac and need no SI. See [Your SI and privacy](privacy.md).
