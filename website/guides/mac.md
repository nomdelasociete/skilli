# Your Mac

The things you do to your Mac itself, by name from the launcher, in your
words with Ask ("pause the music"), or on a key of your own. What cannot
be undone (Restart, Empty Trash…) asks first.

## Commands

| Area | Commands |
| --- | --- |
| Power and screen | Lock Screen, Sleep, Sleep Displays, Restart, Shut Down, Log Out, Keep Awake |
| Appearance and Finder | Toggle Dark Mode, Toggle Hidden Files, Show Desktop, Eject All Disks, Empty Trash |
| Apps | Quit All Apps, Quit Other Apps, Hide Other Apps, Show All Apps |
| Sound | Play / Pause, Next Track, Previous Track, Volume Up, Volume Down, Mute / Unmute, Set Volume (`volume 30`), Sound Output, Microphone, Mute / Unmute Microphone |
| Connections | Turn Wi-Fi On, Turn Wi-Fi Off, Bluetooth Devices, Test Internet Speed |
| Tools | Timer, Pick a Color, System Info, Clean Keyboard |

**Sound Output** and **Bluetooth Devices** list your speakers, AirPods
and displays: ↩ switches to one, or connects it. **Clean Keyboard** turns
every key off for a minute so you can wipe it.

## Keep Awake

`awake 2h` or "keep awake until 6pm" in the launcher, or plain **Keep
Awake** until you turn it off. A cup shows in the menu bar and at the
right of the launcher's field, with the time left; click it to turn Keep
Awake off. Typing `awake` again shows it running: ↩ turns it off, ⌘K adds
an hour.

## Timers

`timer 10 min tea`: it counts down in the menu bar and rings with a card.

## Reminders

- **Remind Me**: `remind me to call Camille at 9am`, or ask "remind me in
  an hour and a half". Places from your contact card work too ("when I
  get home").
- **`todo` in the launcher**: your reminders; check one off from there,
  ⌘⌫ deletes it (⌘Z brings it back).
- Today's reminders can show in the menu bar (Settings → Reminders).

## Apps you stopped using

**Auto Quit** (Settings → Windows → *Apps You Stopped Using*) quits apps
you have not used for a while, after a delay you choose. Never the app in
front, never one with unsaved work, playing sound or in a call. Add apps
to *Never quit* to keep them open.

## Running apps

Type `kill` and an app's name to quit it with its helpers, or a port
number (`kill 3000`) to stop what listens on it. ⇧↩ forces it. A running
app's row in the launcher says "· running", and its ⌘K has Quit and
Force Quit.

`brew` in the launcher searches, installs, upgrades and uninstalls
Homebrew formulae and casks.
